Research

Continuous ECG Monitoring with a Loose T‑Shirt

MobiDx 2026 · MobiCom workshop

Loose clothing was long considered unusable for ECG because the fabric moves with the body. The shirt's own weight and a little sweat hold a thin kirigami sensor to the back, and a hybrid electrode senses motion so it can be filtered out.

92.7% of full waveforms at rest · 84.5% of heartbeats while walking

PDF · Paper ACM

Noninvasive Optical Glucose Sensing

Scientific Reports 2025 · Sensors 2023 · UbiComp 2022

A portable box that estimates glucose levels from how the palm rotates polarized light, with a light trap that rejects stray reflections. Tested in a 363-day home study with 30 participants, run with the Barbara Davis Center for Diabetes.

Over 95% of predictions clinically accurate or acceptable

PDF · Clinical Study PDF · Review PDF · Poster Paper

Computational Fabrics for Health

IMWUT 2025 · MobiSys 2024 · ICORR 2025

Sensors made from conductive fabric and built into everyday textiles: a pillowcase that tracks heart rate, breathing and sleep posture, Joey for kangaroo mother care of newborns, and a fabric sensor for hand-muscle rehabilitation.

My role Built the pillow's sensing prototype, developed Joey's Android app, advised on the hand-muscle sensors
